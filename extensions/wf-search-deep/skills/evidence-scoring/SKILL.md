# Evidence scoring

Score relevance, directness, authority, recency, independence, and evidence
quality using the wf-research result contract. Keep primary evidence separate
from inference and community observation. Discard duplicates or weak items
with a reason, surface contradictions, and replan only the missing claim or
modality. A successful download or transcript is evidence of access, not proof
that its content is true.
